WELCOME TO GLASSHOUSE

A little world in four steps.

  1. 01

    Pick your glass

    Choose a globe, an apothecary jar, or a geometric vessel below the preview.

  2. 02

    Plant something lovely

    Tap a piece in the collection. Drag it inside your terrarium. Select it to change its size, mirror it, or remove it.

  3. 03

    Set the mood

    Try a backdrop, adjust the soil, light, and moisture, and open or close the lid.

  4. 04

    Keep your little world

    Your design saves on this device automatically. Save an image to take it with you. Undo works whenever you need it.
